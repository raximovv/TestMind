How it works

Why TestMind?

Built for Uzbek teenagers, in Uzbek.

Based on a scientific model

The items are drawn from the Big Five, the most extensively tested model of personality in psychology.

Written in natural Uzbek

The items are not a literal translation — they are written in the language teenagers actually use day to day.

Anonymous

The test asks for neither name nor age. Responses are stored without any identifier that could be traced back to the person who gave them, and are used only to improve the instrument.

Completely free

No payment, no subscription, no registration.
