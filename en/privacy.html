Privacy

A short, plainly written account of how TestMind handles your data.

What is stored

When the test is completed, only anonymous data is stored: five trait scores, the name of the result, how long the test took, and the device type (phone or computer). It is held in a private spreadsheet and used only to improve the test.

Age and the figure you choose

Before the result is shown we ask your age and which historical figure you would like to see with it — the male one or the female one. Those two values are stored alongside the anonymous result and used only for aggregate analysis, such as understanding which ages use the test most. Neither a date of birth nor a birth year is requested. No name is needed to take the test. The figure you pick changes only the historical illustration and its description: your scores and your character stay exactly the same.

What is never asked

The test asks for neither your name nor your age, so neither is stored anywhere. No registration is required either: you press "Take the test" and the first item appears.

Your responses

When the test is completed, your item-level responses are stored without a name. The record contains no name, no age and no email address.

The record is given a separate random identifier. That identifier is never linked to your other data — including any email address you may have left. Because of this, the stored responses cannot be traced back to the person who gave them.

Why we keep them: the TestMind items have not yet been validated in an Uzbek adolescent sample, and only real responses can show which items work. The data is never shared or sold.

If you leave an email address

Leaving your email address on the result page is optional. It is sent only when you have typed it in yourself and pressed the button; nothing is sent merely by opening the page.

If you do leave one, it is stored together with the name of your result — which means that record is not anonymous. We use it only to send you the guide you asked for. We never share or sell your email address.

You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to the address below. If you are under 18, talk to a parent or teacher before leaving an email address.

On your device

If you stop the test halfway, your answers are held temporarily in this device’s browser storage so that you can continue. They are deleted automatically once the result appears.

Other sites

TestMind loads no advertising, no tracking scripts and no external fonts — it requests nothing from other sites. The single outbound request is the one described above, sending anonymous data to a private Google spreadsheet.

About the result

The result is advisory and is not a medical or scientific conclusion. Do not treat it as the sole basis for choosing a career or a field of study.

Age

The test is designed for school students aged 13–18. Those under 18 are advised to take it in consultation with a parent or teacher.
